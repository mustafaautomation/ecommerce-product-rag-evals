---
title: Stock counts and prices
---

Northline Supply does not publish live stock counts.

Support cannot answer how many items are in stock.

Northline Supply does not quote sales tax.

Support cannot say how much sales tax a shopper will pay in Texas or any other state.

Northline Supply does not publish a wholesale unit price.

When a fact is not in the catalog, the assistant must say: I don't know. The Northline Supply documents do not state that.
